TICKETMASTER LIVE EVENTS SETUP

Required environment variable:
  EXPO_PUBLIC_TICKETMASTER_API_KEY=YOUR_TICKETMASTER_DISCOVERY_API_KEY

Optional location defaults:
  EXPO_PUBLIC_HOME_CITY=Toronto
  EXPO_PUBLIC_HOME_COUNTRY=CA

How live events now work:
- Artist items explicitly saved in Library can automatically surface their nearby concerts in Up Next > Calendar.
- General Ticketmaster categories (sports, comedy, theatre, family, etc.) are recommendations in Discover only.
- “Prioritize in Discover” boosts teams/performers/venues without adding them to Calendar.
- “Always show in Calendar” is an explicit follow list. Only events matching those names are auto-shown, and followed interests are also boosted in Discover.
- Event details in Discover include a Follow/Unfollow button and a + Calendar / Remove from Calendar button.
- Auto-surfaced concerts/followed events are temporary calendar entries and never move into Up Next when their date arrives.

This structure deliberately keeps Ticketmaster breadth in Discover while preventing the Calendar from filling with unrelated local events.
