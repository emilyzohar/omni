Music/manual/calendar update

- Music can be saved as a general manual item with no subcategory.
- iTunes Music search results are automatically Music > Album.
- Artist, Song, Concert remain true Music subcategories.
- Concert can be entered manually with any date, including past dates.
- Artist, Song, Album/general Music can have optional dates and therefore calendar associations.
- Expanded generic Library sections group items by subcategory; uncategorized items appear under General.
- Recommended badge is heart-only.
